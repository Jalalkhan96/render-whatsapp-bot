# Webcomic Platforms Roundup — legal places to read webtoons & comics

Fact-checked October 2026. Pricing changes often — confirm in the app before paying.
Every platform below is official and pays its creators. That is the whole point.

## Quick comparison

| Platform | Content focus | Cost model | Free tier | Offline reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WEBTOON (webtoons.com) | Korean webtoons, all genres + indie Canvas | Free w/ ads; Fast Pass / Daily Pass for early access | Very generous — most series free if you wait | Yes, in-app for many series |
| Tapas | Webcomics + web novels, creator community | Freemium — "Ink" currency, no subscription | First episodes free; earn Ink via ads/surveys | Limited |
| Manta | Romance / romance-fantasy webtoons | Subscription ≈ $4/month, no per-episode coins | A few episodes free; free daily passes | In-app |

| INKR Comics | Manga + manhwa + webtoons, exclusives | Free w/ in-app purchases | Free tier (account required) | In-app |
| Netcomics | Manhwa + novels | Rent-or-buy with Netcomics Coin | Free section; first chapters free | In-app |

| MANGA Plus (Shueisha) | Manga (not webtoon, but adjacent) | Free | Free, ad-supported | Limited |

## The details

**WEBTOON** — the biggest name (190M+ installs). Most series are free with ads; impatient readers buy Fast Pass to read ahead or use Daily Pass on completed series. WEBTOON and Tapas are the most generous with free reading among major platforms, and both allow limited offline episode downloads. **Canvas** is its free indie-publishing side — thousands of creator-owned series, fully free.

**Tapas** — 100k+ stories, comics plus novels. First episodes of a series are usually free; the rest unlock with Ink, which you buy or earn by watching ads and doing sponsored tasks — no monthly subscription needed.

**Manta** — the "binge" option: one subscription (≈ $3.99–4/month) unlocks the whole catalog, no coins or keys. Heavily romance / romance-fantasy focused, with free daily passes so you can sample before paying.

**Tappytoon** — licensed manhwa with a "Time Till Free" system: wait and episodes unlock at no cost, or pay to read now (free app, in-app purchases).

**Lezhin Comics** — one of the oldest legal manhwa platforms; coin-based pay-per-episode with regular free-episode events. Strong in mature romance and BL.

**INKR Comics** — manga, manhwa and webtoons with its own exclusives and a recommendation engine; free with in-app purchases, but you must create an account to read.

**Netcomics** — long-running manhwa/novel platform. Browse the Free tab; paid chapters can be rented (cheaper) or bought with Netcomics Coin.

**GlobalComix** — a marketplace rather than a single publisher: indie creators and publishers set their own prices, so there is a big free shelf next to paid titles. Good hunting ground for indie gems.

**MANGA Plus** — Shueisha's official free manga app (30M+ installs). Manga rather than webtoon, but if you like the format, it is the legal free home of Shonen Jump series.

## Reading free, legally — the playbook

1. **Wait it out.** WEBTOON, Tapas and Tappytoon all unlock episodes free over time — patience is a valid payment method.
2. **Use the free shelves.** Tapas "Free to read" filter, Netcomics Free tab, GlobalComix free comics, WEBTOON Canvas.
3. **Earn the currency.** Tapas Ink via ads/surveys; Manta daily passes; Lezhin free-episode events.
4. **Offline = the official app.** Download episodes inside the app where offered — never a third-party "webtoon downloader." Those rip creators off and are usually malware with extra steps.

*Checked October 2026. Prices and catalogs shift — the app itself is the source of truth.*
